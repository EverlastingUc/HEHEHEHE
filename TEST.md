I am an independent developer, researcher, and builder interested in understanding how things work — and creating things that do not exist yet.

My interests span programming language design, operating systems, computational physics, artificial intelligence, software architecture, and experimental engineering.

I believe in building from fundamentals, questioning assumptions, and turning ambitious ideas into functional systems.
