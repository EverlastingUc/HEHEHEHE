# 01 — IDENTITY

I am an independent developer, researcher, and builder interested in understanding how things work — and creating things that do not exist yet.

My interests span:

- Programming language design
- Operating system development
- Systems programming
- Computational physics
- Artificial intelligence
- Mathematics and theoretical exploration
- Software architecture
- Algorithms and data structures
- Experimental engineering

My approach is simple:

> Don't just use technology. Understand it. Engineer it. Expand it.

**Core philosophy:** Curiosity drives discovery. Mathematics provides structure. Engineering turns ideas into reality.
